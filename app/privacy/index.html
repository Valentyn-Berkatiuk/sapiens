Sapiens

Privacy Policy

Sapiens has no account and no sign-in. What you read and how you answer stays on this device, and this version sends nothing anywhere.

Effective 22 September 2026. This is the same text the app shows under Settings › Legal, generated from it.

The short version

Sapiens does not ask you to register and has no account to attach anything to. Every lesson ships inside the download, and the review schedule is worked out on the device that shows it to you.

Nothing leaves it. This version sends no usage data and makes no network calls of its own; a purchase goes to Apple, as every App Store purchase does.

What is stored, and where

The app keeps which lessons you have opened and finished, how you rated each recall card, the review dates that follow from those ratings, your streak, and your settings — theme, motion, reminder hour, cards per session.

All of it is written to the app's own storage on this device. Another app cannot read it. If you have iPhone or iCloud backup switched on, that storage is included in your own backup, under your Apple Account and Apple's terms — we have no access to it there either.

What is never collected

No name, no email address, no phone number, no contacts, no location, no photos, no microphone, no advertising identifier, no device fingerprint. Nothing is shared with data brokers, and nothing is used to track you across other companies’ apps or sites.

Which lesson you read, how you answered it and what you are finding hard stay on this device.

Reminders

If you turn the daily reminder on, iOS schedules it locally and the text is composed on the device. Nothing is sent to us or through a push server. Turning it off in Settings removes the pending reminder.

Purchases

Subscriptions and one-off purchases are processed by Apple. Payment details go to Apple and are never seen by this app. The app is told only whether an entitlement is currently active.

Apple's own privacy policy governs that transaction.

Your rights

You can erase everything from Settings › Data. It takes effect immediately and cannot be undone.

Erasure is handled by that control rather than by a request to us, because everything the app holds is on this device. For a copy of it, write to us at the contact address below and we will tell you how to take it.

Children

Sapiens is not directed at children under 13 and collects nothing from anyone, regardless of age.

Changes

If this policy changes, the effective date at the top changes with it, and a material change is shown in the app rather than left for you to find.
